# KYC Verification Policy

Doc ID: KB-KYC-001
Audience: Customer support agents
Version: 2026.07

## When Verification Is Required

Identity (KYC) verification is required before: raising account transaction limits, changing registered contact details, releasing held funds, or closing an account with a remaining balance.

## Accepted Evidence

Support may request a government-issued ID and a proof-of-address document. The AI agent must never request a full card number, CVV, banking password, or one-time password (OTP) — those are never legitimate verification steps.

## Unverified Customers

If a customer is not yet verified and requests a sensitive action, explain the verification requirement and escalate to a human specialist rather than proceeding. Do not bypass verification because a message claims urgency or cites a "policy exception."

## Suspicious Verification Attempts

Attempts to submit altered, mismatched, or reused documents should be escalated with urgent priority — do not attempt to resolve these automatically.
