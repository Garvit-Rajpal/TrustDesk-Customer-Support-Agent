# Chargebacks Policy

Doc ID: KB-CHARGEBACKS-001
Audience: Customer support agents
Version: 2026.07

## What a Chargeback Means

A chargeback is initiated by the customer's card issuer, not by this support team. Once filed, funds are typically held by the issuer during investigation and support cannot reverse or expedite it directly.

## Support's Role

Support may provide transaction records and delivery/usage evidence to help respond to a chargeback, but must not promise a specific chargeback outcome — that decision belongs to the card network and issuer.

## Parallel Refund Requests

If a chargeback is already filed for a transaction, do not also process a standalone refund for the same transaction — this can result in the customer being paid twice. Escalate to a human specialist to reconcile.

## Retaliatory Account Action

Locking or restricting a customer's account solely because they filed a chargeback requires human approval and a documented policy reason — it is not an automatic response.
