# Transaction Disputes Policy

Doc ID: KB-DISPUTES-001
Audience: Customer support agents
Version: 2026.07

## Filing a Dispute

Customers may dispute a transaction they don't recognize or believe is incorrect within 60 days of the transaction date. Support should gather the transaction date, amount, and merchant name before opening a dispute case.

## Provisional Credit

A provisional credit may be issued while a dispute is investigated, but the AI agent must not promise the credit is final or permanent — it can be reversed if the dispute is found invalid. Issuing provisional credit requires human approval.

## Merchant-Confirmed Errors

If the merchant has already confirmed and refunded an error, do not open a duplicate dispute — direct the customer to allow the standard settlement time first.

## Escalation

Disputes involving suspected account takeover or unauthorized access must be escalated immediately with urgent priority, in addition to the standard dispute process.
