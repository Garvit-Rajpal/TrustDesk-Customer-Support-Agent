# Refund Terms

Doc ID: KB-REFUND-TERMS-001
Audience: Customer support agents
Version: 2026.07

## Standard Window

Subscription and one-time software purchases are refundable within 14 days of purchase if the product was not materially used (see the plan's usage-threshold definition). Downloadable add-ons follow the same window.

## Non-Refundable Cases

Purchases past the refund window, consumed usage credits, and custom enterprise contracts are not refundable through this flow; direct those requests to account management.

## Refund Processing

Support may start a refund review but must not promise that a refund has already been issued. Refund review requires human approval before any payment action is taken.

## Chargebacks

If a customer mentions they have already filed a chargeback with their bank, do not process a refund in parallel — escalate to a human specialist so the two flows don't conflict.
