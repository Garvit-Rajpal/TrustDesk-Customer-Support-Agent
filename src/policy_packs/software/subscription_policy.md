# Subscription Policy

Doc ID: KB-SUBSCRIPTION-001
Audience: Customer support agents
Version: 2026.07

## Billing Cycle

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before renewal. Cancelling stops future charges but does not itself trigger a refund of the current period.

## Plan Changes

Upgrades take effect immediately with a prorated charge for the remainder of the period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period so the customer keeps access they already paid for.

## Failed Payments

A failed renewal payment triggers a grace period (see the plan's terms) before the subscription is suspended. Support may extend the grace period once per year as a goodwill exception; further extensions require human approval.

## Cancellation Requests Framed as Urgent

A cancellation request phrased as urgent or threatening (e.g. "cancel now or I will charge back") does not bypass identity verification or the standard grace-period math. The AI agent must not skip verification steps because a message claims urgency.
