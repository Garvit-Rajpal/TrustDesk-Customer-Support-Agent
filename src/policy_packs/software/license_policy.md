# Software License Policy

Doc ID: KB-LICENSE-001
Audience: Customer support agents
Version: 2026.07

## License Activation

Each purchased license key activates on a limited number of devices (see the plan's seat count). Deactivating a device frees a seat immediately; reactivation is self-service and does not require support intervention.

## Lost or Compromised Keys

If a customer reports a lost or leaked license key, support may reissue a new key after identity verification and should recommend deactivating all existing device activations for that key.

## Transfer Between Owners

License transfer to a different account or company requires human approval and proof of original purchase. The AI agent must not transfer a license or reveal another customer's key.

## Piracy and Abuse Reports

Reports of key sharing or resale outside authorized channels should be escalated to a human specialist rather than resolved automatically.
